# Reflection: Platforms in an Increasingly Agentic World

> Personal reflection — 2026-06-03

In an increasingly agentic world, the things that form the **foundations of agentic workflows** are:

- **Platforms with clear boundaries** and **clearly defined needs.**
- **Servers.**
- **Documentation.**
- **Domain expertise** — becomes critical.
- ...as well as the need for **generalists.**

**That middle layer disappears.**

Implications:

- We need to consider **making more platforms.**
- **Identity** is one of those platforms.
- **The unlock is greater.**
