- **Testing for Reliability:** Feature Management & Experimentation, Resilience Testing, AI SRE, AI Test Automation

**How is the platform different?**

- **Modular Platform** — choose best-of-breed modules, combine for an integrated experience, 100+ integrations.
- **Enterprise Scale** — standardize using Harness templates, scale pipelines as needed, reduce maintenance burden by 90%+.
- **Embedded Security & Governance** — org-wide guardrails via Policy-as-Code, human-in-the-loop through Approvals, fine-grained RBAC.
