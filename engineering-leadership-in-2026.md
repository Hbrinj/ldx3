## Notes

- Goal of the talk: learn **how orgs are changing** and **what those changes mean for engineering management as a role.**
- There's a **weird stretch happening in the leadership layer.**
- **The Great Flattening:** orgs are **collapsing in** and **swapping headcount for GPUs.**
  - But the trend was really **driven by over-bloating during the pandemic** (over-hiring), not purely an AI story.

### Slide: "The Pyramid" → "The Plateau" (before/after org shape)

**Before · 2022 — "The Pyramid"**
- **Five layers · 1:7 span · 32% of headcount in management.**
- Structure: CEO → VP → DIR → Manager layer → ICs (L1–L5).
- Span of control **1:7.**

**After · 2026 — "The Plateau"**
- **Three layers · 1:25 span · ICs paired with AI agents.**
- Structure: CEO → LEAD → (human ICs + AI agents + lead/staff ICs).
- Span of control **1:25.**
- Quote on slide: *"Three layers between intern and CEO."* — **Mark Zuckerberg, Meta internal memo, 2025.**
- Legend distinguishes **Human IC · AI agent · Lead/staff IC.**
